Questions clients ask

  

    
How long does it take?
Basic invitations are usually ready in 1–2 days. Premium and Luxury websites take 3–7 days depending on revisions.

    
Can the invitation be in Arabic and English?
Yes. We can design bilingual invitations, with Arabic laid out right-to-left.

    
How do guests RSVP?
Premium and Luxury packages include an RSVP form. Luxury adds a guest list with personalised invitation links for each family.

    
Can we download our attendee list?
Yes. Corporate packages let you export every registration to Excel or CSV at any time, including custom questions such as job title or session choice.

    
Who can see attendee data?
Only your team and ours. Registration details are used for your event only, and we can delete them after the event on request.

    
Can you invoice our company?
Yes. We issue a quotation and invoice in your company's name for corporate orders.

    
How do I pay?
A 50% deposit confirms your order and the balance is paid before the final link goes live.
